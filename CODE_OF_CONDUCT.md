# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully, regardless of background, identity, experience, or disability. Give specific, constructive technical feedback. Welcome questions and acknowledge mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and publishing private information are unacceptable in project spaces and when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict or ban participants in proportion to the behavior. For reports, contact the repository maintainer privately through [their profile](https://github.com/ysj101); do not publish personal information in an issue. Maintainers will respect confidentiality and consider context and affected participants when deciding an appropriate response. Reports concerning a maintainer may also be escalated through GitHub's reporting tools.
